uses its headers from a temporary build directory. The SDK is not vendored in

Build and corresponding source
------------------------------
The MioNo source and build workflow are published at:
https://github.com/Nina2333520/Mio
The workflow fetches JUCE 8.0.7 and the exact ASIO SDK archive listed above.
Those upstream sources, together with this repository's Source/, CMakeLists.txt,
and .github/workflows/build.yml, are the corresponding source/build inputs for
the generated Windows binary. Retain this notice and both license texts with
redistributed builds.
